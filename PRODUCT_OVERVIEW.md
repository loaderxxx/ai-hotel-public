# AI Hotel — Product Overview

STATUS: CONCEPT / VALIDATION PREP
DATE: 2026-10-05

## Product position

AI Hotel is a two-sided AI operating layer for hospitality.

It connects:
1. the hotel management and staff operating surface;
2. Personal AI Hotel for the guest;
3. a shared governed orchestration layer.

## Hotel-side product

Primary users:
- hotel owner;
- general manager;
- hotel director.

Secondary users:
- front office;
- reservations;
- housekeeping;
- maintenance;
- revenue/commercial;
- finance;
- procurement;
- security;
- F&B;
- guest-experience teams.

Core use cases:
- Director Daily Brief;
- exception inbox;
- incident orchestration;
- remote delegation;
- task verification;
- SOP copilot;
- approval queue;
- operational Q&A.

## Guest-side product — Personal AI Hotel

Personal AI Hotel turns natural-language guest requests into verified hotel outcomes.

Core use cases:
- pre-arrival preferences and transfer;
- breakfast/restaurant requests;
- housekeeping and amenities;
- room service;
- maintenance;
- spa/gym;
- taxi/transport;
- late checkout;
- local recommendations and activities;
- invoice/luggage/departure coordination;
- multilingual communication.

## Shared platform

The same operational backbone should support both sides:
- hotel capability registry;
- identity and context;
- event/intent routing;
- workflows;
- permission policies;
- integrations;
- task delegation;
- verification;
- audit;
- memory.

## Research-informed starting point

Begin with approved property information, one supervised service-request category and a manager exception brief. Compare these workflows with the hotel's existing tools before commissioning new integration work.

Current hospitality products already overlap with messaging, task management and AI assistance. The evaluation must demonstrate an advantage in actual service outcomes, operating effort or fit; these features alone do not establish uniqueness.

For a concrete discussion proposal, see [Address Beach Resort](proposals/address/PROPOSAL.md). The property is a proposed evaluation setting, not an agreed customer.

## Initial differentiation hypothesis

AI Hotel should not compete as another single-purpose chatbot.

The hypothesis to validate is that the strongest value comes from:
- cross-department orchestration;
- multi-step outcome management;
- controlled autonomy;
- verified completion;
- one operational state shared between hotel-side and guest-side experiences;
- property-specific SOP and capability grounding.

## Validation gate

Do not build a broad platform until real pilots validate:
- repeated hotel-management pain;
- repeated guest use;
- accessible operational data;
- measurable impact;
- staff operating fit;
- trust in bounded AI actions;
- credible commercial value.
