# AI Hotel — Director Pitch

## One-line pitch

AI Hotel helps a hotel director run operations remotely by turning scattered hotel information into a single AI-managed command surface.

## Problem

Hotel information is usually scattered across:
- PMS;
- chats and messengers;
- guest messages;
- maintenance requests;
- housekeeping updates;
- spreadsheets;
- revenue reports;
- finance reports;
- staff calls;
- shift handovers.

The director becomes the human integration layer.

## What changes

AI Hotel creates one management surface:
- what needs attention;
- what changed;
- what is late;
- who owns the next action;
- what needs approval;
- what risks revenue, service or reputation.

## Example director questions

- What needs my attention right now?
- What changed since yesterday?
- Which guest issues are still unresolved?
- Why is this room blocked?
- What is hurting revenue this week?
- Which tasks are late?
- What should I approve?
- Assign this and tell me when it is completed.

## Pilot proposal

1. Map the current director workflow.
2. Identify 2–3 high-value workflows.
3. Use safe exports or read-only integrations first.
4. Run a bounded pilot.
5. Measure operational impact.

## Safe automation promise

AI starts by observing, summarizing, recommending and preparing actions. Execution begins only with low-risk approved workflows. Sensitive actions remain human-approved.

## Meeting close

Show us one normal working day and one bad day at the hotel. We will identify where AI can remove manual coordination first.
