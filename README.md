# AI Hotel

**AI Hotel is a two-sided AI operating layer for hospitality.**

It connects hotel operations and the guest experience through one governed orchestration backbone.

## Proposal for Address

[Read the proposal for Address Beach Resort and Address Hotels + Resorts](proposals/address/PROPOSAL.md).

The proposed first evaluation combines approved guest information, one service-request workflow and a duty-manager exception brief. It starts at one property and measures value against the current process.

This is an independent discussion proposal, not an announced partnership or an active hotel deployment.

## Two product surfaces

### AI Hotel for Management
A director/owner operating layer that turns fragmented hotel data, tasks, incidents, SOPs and approvals into one command surface.

Core concepts:
- Director Copilot
- Operations Orchestrator
- exception-first management
- verified delegation
- bounded autonomy
- auditability

### Personal AI Hotel
A personal AI concierge/manager for the guest.

The guest should be able to say what they want in natural language:

> "Breakfast at 9, housekeeping after 14:00, a car at 11, and please ask for late checkout."

The system should understand the request, coordinate the relevant hotel teams and connected services, track each action, verify what actually happened and return a clear status.

**Tell the hotel once. Your AI takes care of the rest.**

## Shared platform

Both surfaces use a common governed layer for:
- identity and stay/property context;
- hotel capability registry;
- intent/event routing;
- task and action orchestration;
- integrations;
- permissions and approvals;
- verification;
- audit trail;
- multilingual interaction;
- operational and guest preference memory.

## Product languages

The product is designed for:
- English
- Russian

Repository documentation is maintained in English.

## What AI Hotel is not

- not a replacement PMS at the first stage;
- not a generic hotel chatbot;
- not uncontrolled autonomous operation;
- not a promise that every hotel process can be automated.

## Validation approach

We start with real workflows and bounded pilots.

Hotel-side:
1. Director Daily Brief
2. Incident / service issue orchestration
3. Cross-department task delegation and verification

Guest-side:
1. hotel information
2. housekeeping and amenities
3. breakfast / restaurant requests
4. taxi / transfer
5. late checkout
6. maintenance issue
7. human concierge escalation

The goal is to prove measurable value before broad platform build.

## Status

Concept / validation stage. Public desk research has informed the product and the Address proposal; hotel interviews, internal system validation and live pilot results remain outstanding.

Descriptions of the platform are intended behavior and proposed capabilities, not a list of deployed integrations.

This repository contains public product documentation. Internal operating methods, private data, credentials and proprietary private-core material are intentionally excluded.
