# Personal AI Hotel

## One-line definition

Personal AI Hotel is a guest-side AI agent that turns natural-language requests into verified hotel outcomes.

## Promise

**Tell the hotel once. Your AI takes care of the rest.**

The guest should not need to know which department, phone number, form or hotel system is responsible.

## Example

Guest:

> Tomorrow I want breakfast at 9, a quiet table, a car at 11 to the marina, housekeeping after 14:00, and please ask for late checkout.

Personal AI Hotel should:
1. understand the different requests;
2. identify dependencies and hotel rules;
3. request approval only where needed;
4. execute connected actions or route structured work to staff;
5. track each action;
6. verify the result;
7. show the guest what is CONFIRMED, PENDING, NEEDS APPROVAL or FAILED;
8. update the plan if something changes.

## Guest journey

### Before arrival
- airport transfer;
- early check-in request;
- room preferences;
- family arrangements;
- restaurant/spa planning;
- special occasion setup;
- arrival information.

### During stay
- breakfast;
- housekeeping;
- amenities;
- maintenance;
- room service;
- restaurant;
- spa/gym;
- transport;
- late checkout;
- luggage;
- recommendations;
- activities;
- human concierge escalation.

### Departure / after stay
- checkout coordination;
- luggage;
- invoice/receipt;
- transfer;
- lost-and-found;
- feedback;
- permitted preference retention.

## Experience principles

- voice-first and chat-first;
- one conversational surface;
- English and Russian as first-class languages;
- minimal clarification;
- transparent status;
- no fabricated confirmations;
- human concierge always available;
- hotel brand can own the experience;
- the agent becomes more useful as stay context accumulates.

## Product hypothesis

The differentiation is not simply AI messaging.

The hypothesis is deeper orchestration:
- multi-intent planning;
- cross-department execution;
- dependency management;
- verified task state;
- guest preference memory;
- proactive plan maintenance;
- bounded authority;
- shared hotel-side and guest-side operational context.

This is a hypothesis to validate through real hotel pilots.
